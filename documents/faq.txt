Company: TechCorp
Product: CloudStore - a cloud storage service

Q: How much storage do I get on the free plan?
A: The free plan includes 10GB of storage.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, and PayPal.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". You'll receive an email within 2 minutes.

Q: Is my data encrypted?
A: Yes, all data is encrypted at rest and in transit using AES-256 encryption.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your data is kept for 30 days after cancellation.
